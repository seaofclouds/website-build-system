These posts are the working record of turning [automerge.org](https://automerge.org)'s
build system into this template — what was removed, what was generalised, and the handful
of bugs that only appear when you try to use something as a starting point rather than as
a finished site.

They're also the demonstration of how a section works here: dated pages, a generated
index, an RSS feed, and previous/next links, all from putting Markdown files in a folder.
Delete `content/blog` if your site doesn't need one.
