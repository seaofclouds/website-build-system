This is the shape Ink & Switch essays use, and the one the figure vocabulary in content.css was designed against — see /styleguide/ for everything it can do. Requires a `date` in the page's frontmatter, for the byline. `authors` is optional. Two kinds of navigation, at the two ends. {{toc}} at the head builds a contents list from the page's own h2s, and emits nothing if there are fewer than two — that answers "where am I inside this". Older/newer at the foot answer "what else is near this one". Note they aren't the same claim as the docs template's previous/next, which follow a running order you're meant to read in sequence. These follow dates, which is only a statement that more of these exist.
